# Dan Pu

I am a Lecturer with the School of Statistics and Data Science and the Joint Laboratory of Data Science and Business Intelligence at Southwestern University of Finance and Economics (SWUFE). I received my Ph.D. in Statistics from Xiamen University (2018.09–2023.06) and my B.S. in Statistics from Chongqing University (2014.09–2018.06).
My research interests include complex network data analysis, spatial econometrics, and spatiotemporal panel data analysis.
